# Platform Comparison: Google vs Meta vs ChatGPT Ads

Data from Northbeam Q1 2026 (1,800 DTC brands), Tinuiti Q2 2026, Calc4Marketers, Ryze AI, InsightIQ, and OpenAI official partner results.

## At a Glance

| Dimension | Google Ads | Meta Ads | ChatGPT Ads |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Core mechanic** | Keyword auction, capture intent | Interest/demo targeting, create demand | Entity confidence, trust-mediated inclusion |
| **User mindset** | "I need to buy X now" | "What's this in my feed?" | "What should I buy / how does this work?" |
| **Best for** | Existing demand, branded search, product-specific | Awareness, retargeting, visual products | Research-mode buyers, new customer acquisition |
| **Avg CPC (healthcare)** | $3.50-8.00 | $1.85-6.40 | $3-5 |
| **Avg CVR** | 3.75% | 0.89% (cold), 5-12% (retargeting) | In testing |
| **Avg CPA (healthcare)** | $55-100 | $60-110 | 15% below Google (vendor claim, single source) |
| **ROAS (health/wellness)** | 3.5-5.0x | 2.0-3.2x | Unknown |
| **Min viable budget** | $10-15/day | $10-20/day | $500/day recommended |
| **Time to signal** | 14-30 days | 30-50 conversions | 30+ days |
| **Tracking maturity** | Mature (GA4, gtag, CAPI) | Mature (Pixel + CAPI) | New (pixel + CAPI just launched) |
| **Availability** | Global | Global | US, UK, Mexico, Brazil, Japan, South Korea (pilot) |

## Google Ads

**Strengths:**
- Highest conversion rate in healthcare (3.75% vs Meta 0.89%)
- Captures existing purchase intent
- Product Shopping ads show price + image directly in results
- Mature conversion tracking and attribution
- Best for need-based products (medical supplies, B2B, services)

**Weaknesses:**
- Higher CPC ($3.50-8.00 in healthcare)
- Requires keyword research and ongoing negative keyword management
- PMax is a black box; Shopping needs clean product feed
- Competitive categories get expensive fast

**When to choose Google first:**
- People search for your product by name (Tegaderm, Mepilex, stoma supplies)
- You have a product feed ready
- You need measurable ROI fast
- Your product is need-based, not impulse

## Meta Ads (Facebook + Instagram)

**Strengths:**
- Cheaper CPC ($1.85-6.40 healthcare)
- Best for visual products and brand storytelling
- Advantage+ Shopping automates audience + creative + placement
- Retargeting CVR 5-12% (much higher than cold)
- UGC and creator content performs well

**Weaknesses:**
- Cold traffic CVR is very low (0.89% healthcare)
- Creative fatigue requires ongoing production
- iOS privacy changes reduced tracking accuracy (need CAPI)
- Medical/health categories have restricted targeting
- Algorithm needs 50+ conversions to exit learning phase

**When to choose Meta first:**
- Product is visual, discovery-driven, or impulse (under $75)
- You have strong creative or UGC capability
- You already have site traffic to retarget
- Brand awareness is the primary goal

## ChatGPT Ads

**Strengths:**
- 15.3% lower CPA than Google paid search (WeightWatchers, OpenAI data)
- 67-93% of visitors are net-new customers
- Users in research/recommendation mode
- Content quality can outrank bigger budgets (not pure auction)
- Pixel + CAPI delivers 57% more conversions, 28% lower CPA

**Weaknesses:**
- No cross-advertiser benchmark published (results are curated vendor case studies)
- Not available in Singapore (pilot: US, UK, Mexico, Brazil, Japan, South Korea)
- Recommended $500/day for stable campaigns
- Cannot see chat content (privacy)
- Cannot influence organic responses
- Ads appear AFTER answers, not before intent

**When to choose ChatGPT Ads:**
- Your buyers are in research mode ("best wound dressing for chronic wounds")
- You have strong content/SEO that AI can reference
- You're in a pilot country
- You have $3,000-5,000/month for a fair test
- Google + Meta are already stable

## The Hybrid Reality

No brand picks one platform. The question is order and allocation:

1. **Google Search** captures people already looking for you
2. **Meta Retargeting** recaptures people who visited but didn't buy
3. **Meta Cold / ASC** creates new demand (only after 1+2 work)
4. **ChatGPT Ads** experiments with AI-driven discovery (waitlist if not in pilot)

For medical/health e-commerce specifically, Google Search has a structural advantage because these are need-based purchases. People don't discover stoma supplies on Instagram. They get a prescription, then search for where to buy.

## False Corroboration Flags

- ChatGPT "15.3% lower CPA" = single OpenAI blog post, one partner (WeightWatchers). Not independently verified.
- "Meta ASC 3.1x vs PMax 2.6x" = Northbeam data cited in two Ecommerce Times articles. Same underlying study.
- Healthcare CPA benchmarks = Calc4Marketers and Studio Close may share a common WordStream-derived source.
