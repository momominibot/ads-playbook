# HSA Compliance for Medical Device Advertising

Singapore Health Sciences Authority (HSA) rules for medical device ads. Non-negotiable for emis.asia.

## Regulatory Framework

- **Health Products Act (HPA)** and Health Products (Medical Devices) Regulations 2010
- **Singapore Code of Advertising Practice (SCAP)** administered by ASAS
- **GN-08 Guidance on Medical Device Advertisements and Sales Promotion**
- MOH-HSA-SMC Joint Circular on Advertisements (2024)

## Core Principles

1. **Do not discourage medical advice.** Ads must not suggest the product replaces professional medical consultation.
2. **No false or misleading claims.** All information must be substantiated.
3. **Comply with SCAP.** All medical advertisements must follow the advertising standards code.
4. **Professional-use devices** cannot be advertised to the general public (only to healthcare professionals).

## What You CAN Say

- Product name, brand, manufacturer
- Specifications: size, quantity, material, dimensions
- Intended use (as registered with HSA)
- Price, availability, delivery information
- General product descriptions ("transparent film dressing", "stoma adhesive paste")
- Compatibility ("fits all standard ostomy bags")
- Storage instructions
- Authenticity ("genuine 3M product")

## What You CANNOT Say

| Prohibited Claim | Why | Safe Alternative |
|---|---|---|
| "Heals wounds faster" | Therapeutic claim, unsubstantiated | "Supports the healing environment" (if registered) |
| "Prevents infection" | Medical claim requiring evidence | "Barrier film protects from external contaminants" |
| "Doctor recommended" | Implies professional endorsement without proof | "Used in hospitals worldwide" (if true, verifiable) |
| "Best wound dressing" | Unsubstantiated superiority | "Popular choice for chronic wound care" |
| "Cures stoma issues" | Cure claim prohibited | "Helps manage stoma care routine" |
| "100% safe" | Absolute safety claim | "Clinically tested" (if substantiated) |
| Before/after photos of wounds | Graphic medical content prohibited | Product-only images, application diagrams |
| "Alternative to seeing a doctor" | Discourages medical advice | "Consult your healthcare professional for wound care advice" |

## Platform-Specific Rules

### Google Ads
- Healthcare keywords may require pre-approval
- No cure/treatment claims in ad copy
- Landing pages must comply with Google healthcare policy
- Some medical device categories restricted
- Use factual product language, not benefit claims

### Meta Ads
- No before/after medical imagery
- No health condition targeting (interest targeting restricted)
- No claims about curing or treating conditions
- Educational content ("how to choose a dressing") is allowed
- Broad audience + product-focused creative is safest

### ChatGPT Ads
- Content must comply with OpenAI advertising policies
- Medical/health category may have additional review
- AI may reference your content in organic responses (separate from ads)

## Landing Page Compliance

- Include "Always consult a healthcare professional for medical advice" disclaimer in footer
- Product descriptions stick to registered intended use
- No customer reviews making health claims (moderate reviews)
- No before/after photos in user-generated content
- Clear product specifications and manufacturer info

## Review Process

Before launching any ad:
1. [ ] No health claims (heal, cure, prevent, treat)
2. [ ] No before/after medical imagery
3. [ ] No unsubstantiated superiority ("best", "#1")
4. [ ] Product name and specs are accurate
5. [ ] Price matches landing page
6. [ ] Landing page has medical disclaimer
7. [ ] No targeting by health condition (Meta)
8. [ ] All claims can be substantiated with documentation

## When in Doubt

- Check HSA GN-08 guidance document
- Review SCAP code at asas.org.sg
- Consult a regulatory affairs professional for high-risk products
- When unsure, remove the claim. Factual product copy always passes.
