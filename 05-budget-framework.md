# Budget Framework

How much to spend, in what order, by business stage. Data from Ryze AI, Nova Pixel, Ecommerce Times, and practitioner benchmarks.

## The First Rule: Don't Spend Until Tracking Works

You cannot optimize what you cannot measure. Before any ad spend:
- [ ] Meta Pixel + CAPI firing correctly (Event Match Quality 7+)
- [ ] Google Ads conversion tracking (purchase with value)
- [ ] GA4 installed and linked
- [ ] Test conversion fired and verified in both platforms
- [ ] Product feed synced (if Shopping/ASC)

**Spending without tracking = lighting money on fire.** You will not know what works.

## Budget by Stage

### Stage 0: Pre-Spend (SGD 0)
- Install all tracking
- Verify with test purchases
- Optimize product pages (load speed, message match, reviews)
- Clean product feed (titles, GTINs, images)
- Build 3-5 creative assets
- Duration: 1-2 weeks

### Stage 1: Validation (SGD 15-25/day, SGD 450-750/month)
**Goal:** Prove that paid traffic converts at an acceptable CPA.

| Platform | Allocation | Daily Budget | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| Google Search | 60-70% | SGD 10-15 | Capture existing demand, measure baseline CVR |
| Meta Retargeting | 30-40% | SGD 5-10 | Recapture site visitors from Google + organic |
| Meta Cold | 0% | SGD 0 | Not yet -- need data first |
| ChatGPT / Other | 0% | SGD 0 | Not yet |

**Exit criteria (move to Stage 2 when):**
- 30+ conversions tracked
- Blended CPA below target (e.g. SGD 50-70 for medical)
- Product page CVR above 1.5%
- At least 2 winning ad creatives identified

### Stage 2: Optimization (SGD 30-60/day, SGD 900-1,800/month)
**Goal:** Improve CPA, scale winning channels.

| Platform | Allocation | Daily Budget | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| Google Search | 50% | SGD 15-30 | Scale winning keywords, add Shopping |
| Google Shopping | 15% | SGD 5-10 | Product feed ads (if feed approved) |
| Meta Retargeting | 20% | SGD 6-12 | Scale DPA, expand audiences |
| Meta Cold (ASC) | 15% | SGD 5-10 | Test UGC creative, broad audience |

**Exit criteria:**
- Blended CPA stable or improving
- Meta ASC achieving 2.5x+ ROAS
- Google Shopping feed approved and profitable
- Creative testing system running (3-5 new/week)

### Stage 3: Scaling (SGD 60-150/day, SGD 1,800-4,500/month)
**Goal:** Expand reach while maintaining efficiency.

| Platform | Allocation | Daily Budget |
|---|---|---|
| Google Search + Shopping | 45% | SGD 27-68 |
| Meta ASC + Retargeting | 40% | SGD 24-60 |
| ChatGPT Ads (if available) | 10% | SGD 6-15 |
| YouTube / Demand Gen | 5% | SGD 3-8 |

### Stage 4: Mature (SGD 150+/day)
**Goal:** Full-funnel, multi-channel, creative-led growth.
- Google: 40% (Search, Shopping, PMax)
- Meta: 35% (ASC, retargeting, awareness)
- ChatGPT/AI: 15%
- YouTube/TikTok: 10%

## Budget as Percentage of Revenue (Ezra Firestone)

"Investing between 10% and 30% of your store's revenue in ads is one of those things where you've got to be willing to consistently spend over time."

| Revenue/Month | Ad Spend (10-30%) | Platform Split |
|---|---|---|
| SGD 5,000 | SGD 500-1,500 | Stage 1-2 |
| SGD 10,000 | SGD 1,000-3,000 | Stage 2 |
| SGD 30,000 | SGD 3,000-9,000 | Stage 2-3 |
| SGD 50,000+ | SGD 5,000-15,000 | Stage 3-4 |

## The SGD 30/day Question (Answered)

For a medical supplies store with zero tracking baseline:
- SGD 30/day on PMax = too aggressive, no conversion history
- SGD 30/day on Google Search = reasonable, but split: SGD 20 Search + SGD 10 Meta retargeting
- Expected at SGD 30/day: 10-20 clicks/day (medical CPC SGD 1.50-3.00), 0.1-0.4 conversions/day at 1-2% CVR
- 50 conversions (learning phase) would take 125-500 days at this rate
- **Conclusion:** SGD 30/day is a learning budget, not a scaling budget. Plan for 60-90 days of data before judging.

## What NOT to Do

1. **Don't split SGD 30 across 5 platforms.** SGD 6/day each = no signal on any.
2. **Don't start PMax with zero conversions.** It will waste money.
3. **Don't boost posts.** Meta Boost is not Advantage+ Shopping.
4. **Don't change bids/creatives daily.** Let campaigns run 3-5 days minimum.
5. **Don't kill a campaign after 3 bad days.** Learning phase takes 7-14 days.
6. **Don't scale faster than 20% every 3 days.** Resets learning.
7. **Don't trust in-platform ROAS alone.** Use blended CPA and multi-touch attribution.

## 90-Day Test Budget (Recommended for emis.asia)

| Phase | Days | Daily Budget | Total | Goal |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Tracking + prep | 0-14 | SGD 0 | SGD 0 | Install, verify, optimize pages |
| Google Search test | 15-45 | SGD 15 | SGD 465 | Prove search demand + CVR |
| Add Meta retargeting | 30-60 | SGD 10 | SGD 310 | Recapture warm traffic |
| Optimize + scale | 45-90 | SGD 25 | SGD 1,125 | Scale winners, test creative |
| **Total** | **90 days** | | **~SGD 1,900** | **Baseline + 2 winning channels** |

At SGD 1,900 over 90 days, expect:
- 600-1,200 clicks (medical CPC SGD 1.50-3.00)
- 10-25 conversions (at 1.5-2.5% CVR)
- Blended CPA SGD 76-190 (medical range)
- Data to decide: scale, pivot, or stop
