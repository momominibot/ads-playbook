# Phase 0: Business Foundation Assessment

Before applying any ads strategy, you must know what kind of business you are dealing with. The wrong strategy for the wrong business type burns money. This phase is mandatory and takes 1-2 days.

## The Core Principle

**No ad strategy works for every business.** A medical device store needs Google Search. A crystal pet product needs Meta visual. A B2B reinstatement contractor needs Google Search + LinkedIn. A SaaS needs Google Search + retargeting. Phase 0 determines which playbook applies.

## The 4-Dimension Scoring Model

Score each business on 4 dimensions, 0-25 each, total 0-100.

### Dimension 1: Business Fit (0-25 points)

| Factor | Score | Criteria |
|---|---|---|
| Business model | 0-5 | DTC ecomm (5), B2B service (4), SaaS (3), local service (3), marketplace (2), affiliate (1) |
| Gross margin | 0-5 | >60% (5), 40-60% (4), 25-40% (3), 15-25% (2), <15% (1) |
| Average order value | 0-5 | $50-200 (5), $20-50 (4), $200-500 (3), $10-20 (2), >$500 or <$10 (1) |
| Purchase frequency | 0-5 | Recurring monthly (5), quarterly (4), semi-annual (3), annual (2), one-time (1) |
| Regulatory complexity | 0-5 | Unregulated (5), light compliance (4), moderate (3), heavy (medical/finance) (2), prohibited (0) |

### Dimension 2: Demand Signal (0-25 points)

| Factor | Score | Criteria |
|---|---|---|
| Brand search volume | 0-5 | 1000+ monthly searches (5), 100-1000 (4), 10-100 (3), 1-10 (2), zero (1) |
| Product category search | 0-5 | 10K+ monthly (5), 1K-10K (4), 100-1K (3), 10-100 (2), <10 (1) |
| Existing revenue channels | 0-5 | 3+ channels profitable (5), 2 channels (4), 1 channel stable (3), 1 channel new (2), zero revenue (1) |
| Competitor ad activity | 0-5 | 5+ competitors running ads 30+ days (5), 3-5 (4), 1-2 (3), only tests (2), none (1) |
| Seasonality | 0-5 | Year-round stable (5), mild seasonality (4), moderate (3), strong seasonal (2), one-month wonder (1) |

### Dimension 3: Advertising Readiness (0-25 points)

| Factor | Score | Criteria |
|---|---|---|
| Tracking installed & verified | 0-5 | Pixel+CAPI+Google conversions all firing (5), partial (3), none (0) |
| Landing page quality | 0-5 | Fast (<2s), mobile-optimized, reviews, clear CTA (5), decent (3), broken/slow (0) |
| Product feed quality | 0-5 | GTINs, optimized titles, 1200px images (5), partial (3), none (0) |
| Creative capability | 0-5 | In-house + AI UGC pipeline (5), AI tools only (3), none (0) |
| Email/SMS retention | 0-5 | Flows set up, 20%+ revenue from email (5), basic (3), none (0) |

### Dimension 4: Budget & Risk (0-25 points)

| Factor | Score | Criteria |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly ad budget | 0-5 | $3K+ (5), $1-3K (4), $500-1K (3), $200-500 (2), <$200 (1) |
| Test duration commitment | 0-5 | 90+ days (5), 60 days (4), 30 days (3), 14 days (2), "see if it works" (1) |
| Risk tolerance | 0-5 | Accept 60 days of negative ROI (5), 30 days (3), must be profitable week 1 (0) |
| Owner time available | 0-5 | 5+ hrs/week (5), 2-5 hrs (3), <1 hr (1) |
| Data literacy | 0-5 | Reads dashboards, understands CPA/ROAS (5), basic (3), needs hand-holding (1) |

## Score Interpretation

| Total Score | Readiness | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 80-100 | Ready to scale | Launch Google + Meta simultaneously, $50+/day |
| 60-79 | Ready to test | Start with one platform (highest-fit), $15-30/day, 60-day test |
| 40-59 | Needs foundation | Fix tracking + landing pages first, then $10-15/day test |
| 20-39 | Not ready | 30-60 days of foundation work before any ad spend |
| 0-19 | Don't advertise | Fix business model, product, or channels first |

| Business Type | Primary | Secondary | Skip |
|---|---|---|---|
| DTC ecomm (need-based, medical) | Google Search | Meta Retargeting | TikTok, YouTube |
| DTC ecomm (visual, impulse) | Meta ASC | Google Shopping | LinkedIn |
| DTC ecomm (high AOV >$200) | Google Search | Meta Retargeting | TikTok cold |
| B2B service | Google Search | LinkedIn | Meta, TikTok |
| B2B SaaS | Google Search | Meta Retargeting + LinkedIn | TikTok |
| Local service | Google Search + Maps | Meta local | YouTube, TikTok |
| Wellness/health consumer | Meta ASC | Google Search | TikTok (low ROAS) |
| Fashion/apparel | Meta ASC | Google Shopping | Google Search |
| Home/DIY | Google Search + Shopping | Meta | TikTok |
| Pet products | Meta ASC | Google Shopping | LinkedIn |

## The 7 Questions That Determine Everything

Answer these before spending a dollar:

1. **Do people search for this by name?** (If yes = Google first; if no = Meta first)
2. **Is this a need or a want?** (Need = Google; Want = Meta)
3. **What's the gross margin?** (Below 30% = ads probably won't work; need higher AOV or retention)
4. **Is there a recurring purchase?** (Recurring = can afford higher CPA; one-time = CPA must be <30% of AOV)
5. **Who is the customer?** (Consumer = Meta/Google; Business = Google/LinkedIn)
6. **What does the competitor landscape look like?** (5+ proven ads = validated demand; zero = unproven, riskier)
7. **Can we track it?** (No tracking = no ads. Period.)

## Phase 0 Deliverables

Before moving to Phase 1 (tracking) or Phase 2 (first campaign):

- [ ] 4-dimension score calculated (documented)
- [ ] Business type classified (from matrix above)
- [ ] Platform priority determined
- [ ] 7 questions answered in writing
- [ ] Competitor ad audit completed (see 11-competitive-intelligence.md)
- [ ] Budget + timeline committed (minimum 60 days)
- [ ] Tracking gaps identified (what needs installing)
- [ ] Landing page audit completed (speed, mobile, message match)
- [ ] Go/no-go decision documented
