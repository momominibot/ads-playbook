# Creative & UGC Framework

AI-assisted synthetic UGC and creator content production for paid ads. The "AI ghosting" methodology applied to ad creative.

## Why Creative Matters

Creative is 70%+ of ad performance. You can have perfect targeting, bidding, and tracking, but bad creative kills everything. In 2026, the winning formula is volume + speed: test more creatives faster, kill losers, scale winners.

## The 80/20 Hybrid Model

**80% AI-assisted UGC volume + 20% human hero assets.**

- AI UGC is cheap, fast, and testable ($5-15 per video vs $200-500 for human creators)
- Human hero assets build trust and brand (founder videos, real customer testimonials)
- Use AI to test concepts at volume, then remake winners with real creators

## UGC Ad Formats That Work

### 1. Hook + Demo (15-30s)
```
0-3s: Hook ("Most people use the wrong wound dressing")
3-10s: Problem (dressings that don't stick, cause irritation)
10-20s: Product reveal + demo (Tegaderm application)
20-25s: Benefit summary ("stays on for 7 days, waterproof")
25-30s: CTA ("Shop now, free delivery above SGD 50")
```

### 2. Unboxing / ASMR (10-20s)
- Close-up product unboxing
- Packaging, quality signals
- Text overlays with product name + price
- No voiceover needed (music + text)
- High thumb-stop ratio

### 3. Comparison / VS (20-30s)
- Generic product vs branded product
- Side-by-side visual
- "You get what you pay for" angle
- CTA to branded product page

### 4. Educational / How-To (30-60s)
- "How to choose a wound dressing"
- "3 types of stoma paste explained"
- Establishes authority, drives search
- Best for top-of-funnel and SEO content

### 5. Review / Social Proof (15-20s)
- Text-based customer reviews over product video
- Rating stars, verified purchase badges
- "4.9/5 from 200+ customers"
- Low production cost, high trust

## AI UGC Production Workflow

```
1. Concept (Monday)
├── Pull winning patterns from last week's ads
├── Generate 5-10 hook ideas
├── Pick 3 concepts to test

2. Script (Tuesday)
├── Write 30-60 second script per concept
├── Include hook, problem, solution, CTA
├── Keep language conversational, not corporate

3. Generate (Wednesday)
├── Use Higgsfield/HeyGen/Arcads to generate video
├── Create 2-3 variants per concept (different hooks)
├── Add captions, text overlays, brand logo

4. Review (Thursday)
├── Check for HSA compliance (no health claims)
├── Verify product accuracy
├── Ensure no AI artifacts (weird hands, text glitches)

5. Launch (Friday)
├── Upload to Meta ASC / Google Demand Gen
├── Set $5-10/day test budget
├── Document in creative testing log
```

## Compliance Guardrails for AI UGC

- [ ] No health claims ("heals", "cures", "prevents")
- [ ] No before/after medical imagery
- [ ] No fake testimonials (must be real or clearly labeled "dramatization")
- [ ] AI-generated content must be disclosed where required (Meta "AI Info" label, Google ai_generated attribute)
- [ ] Product names and specs must be accurate
- [ ] No impersonation of real people without consent
- [ ] Medical disclaimers where appropriate

## Creative Performance Metrics

| Metric | Good | Great | Kill |

| CTR (Meta) | 1%+ | 2%+ | <0.5% |
| Thumb-stop (3s view) | 25%+ | 40%+ | <15% |

| Frequency | <3.0 | <2.0 | >4.0 (fatigue) |

## When to Remake a Winner with Human Creator

If an AI UGC creative hits:
- CTR above 2% for 7+ days
- CPA 30%+ below account average
- 100+ conversions attributed

Then:
1. Identify the winning hook/angle
2. Brief a real creator (or film in-house) with the same script
3. Run human version against AI version
4. Keep whichever performs better
5. Use human version as "hero" asset for scaling
