# Ads PlayBook

A data-driven, cost-effective paid advertising playbook for DTC brands, with specific guidance for medical/health e-commerce in regulated markets (Singapore HSA).

Built from Gold Drop methodology research across 22+ expert sources, benchmark aggregators (Northbeam, Tinuiti, Calc4Marketers), platform data (OpenAI, Google, Meta), and practitioner playbooks.

## Who this is for

- DTC founders and growth marketers running on small to medium budgets (SGD 500-5,000/month)
- Medical, health, and wellness e-commerce brands in regulated markets
- Anyone deciding where to spend their first ad dollars across Google, Meta, and emerging AI platforms

## Core principle

**Capture existing demand before creating new demand.** The cheapest customer is the one already searching for what you sell. Google Search converts at 3.75% vs Meta cold traffic at 0.89% in healthcare. Start where intent is highest, then expand.

## Quick start decision tree

```
Do people search for your product by name?
├── YES → Google Search first (brand + product keywords)
│   └── After 30 days data → Meta retargeting
│       └── After retargeting works → Meta cold / Advantage+
└── NO (discovery/impulse product)
    ├── Visual/under $75 → Meta first
    └── High consideration → Google Search (problem keywords) + Meta awareness

ChatGPT Ads? → Join waitlist if not in pilot country. Test only after Google + Meta are stable.
YouTube? → Skip below $50/day. Needs conversion history.
TikTok? → Lowest ROAS in health (1.2-2.0x). Skip unless demo is Gen Z.
```
