# emis.asia Specific Campaign Plan

Wound care and stoma supplies DTC store, Singapore, Shopify Dawn, HSA-regulated medical devices.

## Business Context

- **Store:** emis.asia (EMIS+ / Essential Medical International Supplies)
- **Products:** Wound dressings (3M Tegaderm, Molnlycke Mepilex), stoma care (ConvaTec Stomahesive), skin barriers (3M Cavilon), antiseptics, chronic wound care
- **Price range:** SGD 5-305 (most products SGD 10-50)
- **Market:** Singapore (primary), regional expansion possible
- **Regulation:** HSA medical devices, SCAP advertising code
- **Existing channels:** Shopee (top store SGD 1,232/period, +34.23%), BigSeller (4,049 lifetime orders, ~1,350/month)
- **Shopify:** Zero tracking baseline, gtag + Pixel installed (Pixel ID pending), theme ID 154286882894

## Competitive Position

- **Strengths:** Genuine branded products (3M, ConvaTec, Molnlycke), hospital-trusted brands, Singapore inventory, fast delivery
- **Weaknesses:** No brand recognition, zero paid ads history, limited creative assets, tracking just installed
- **Opportunities:** Low competition for specific product keywords in Singapore, recurring purchase nature (stoma supplies), Shopee traffic can be retargeted
- **Threats:** Amazon.sg, Shopee in-app ads, pharmacy chains (Guardian, Watsons), hospital pharmacies

## Meta Ads Strategy

### Phase 1: Retargeting Only (first 30-60 days)
- Audience: Site visitors from Google + organic + Shopee
- Dynamic Product Ads from catalog
- Creative: product images, reviews, free shipping offer
- Budget: SGD 5-10/day

### Phase 2: ASC Test (after 50+ conversions)
- Broad audience, Singapore
- Creative types to test:
  1. Product unboxing (Tegaderm packaging)
  2. "How to apply" educational (no wound imagery)
  3. Founder/brand story (Essential Medical mission)
  4. Comparison: generic vs branded dressing
  5. Customer review highlight (text-only, no medical claims)
  6. "Hospital-trusted brands delivered to your door"
- Budget: SGD 10-15/day

### Creative Constraints (HSA + Meta)
- No before/after wound photos
- No health claims in captions
- No condition targeting (use broad + creative)
- Educational content works best ("how to choose a wound dressing")
- Product-focused UGC (unboxing, size comparison)

## Risks & Mitigations

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Google Ads disapproval (medical) | Medium | High | Use factual copy, pre-review against HSA rules |
| Meta creative rejection | Medium | Medium | Avoid health claims, use product-only creative |
| Low CVR due to high AOV products | High | Medium | Focus on SGD 10-50 products first, bundle offers |
| Tracking issues (iOS) | High | Medium | CAPI mandatory, use blended CPA not platform ROAS |
| Shopee cannibalization | Medium | Low | Shopify-exclusive bundles, loyalty program |
| Budget too low for learning | High | Medium | 90-day commitment, don't judge before 30 days |
